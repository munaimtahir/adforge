# Creative Repair

`REPAIR_MAP` maps creative failures to narrow stages: keyboard to APP_CAPTURE; raw UI and proof to STORYBOARD; duplicate text and collisions to SCRIPT/TYPOGRAPHY/EDIT_PLAN; CTA duration to EDIT_PLAN; continuity to COMPOSITION/EDIT_PLAN; audio to AUDIO_PRODUCTION; unsupported claims to STRATEGY/SCRIPT. `repair_instruction` preserves canonical identity by default. Existing V1 repair budgeting remains active and must be extended by the integration owner for repeated-identical-failure escalation.
