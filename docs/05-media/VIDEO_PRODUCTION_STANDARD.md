# Video Production Standard

## Default Android app ad
Primary: 20 sec, 9:16.

Indicative structure:
- 0–3s Hook
- 3–7s Pain/problem
- 7–10s Product reveal
- 10–16s Authentic UI/proof
- 16–18s Benefit
- 18–20s CTA

The creative agent may deviate when justified.

## Supported outputs
Ratios: 9:16, 16:9, 1:1  
Durations: 6, 10, 15, 20, 30 seconds

## Hybrid-production rule
Prefer:
AI cinematic footage + authentic app footage + deterministic graphics/audio.

Do not spend video-generation credits on text cards, logos, simple transitions, app UI that can be captured, or deterministic motion graphics.
